ABOUT

How Room by Room works.

Room by Room is an independent home journal. We publish practical guides about furnishing and running a home, written for people who are making a decision this month.

What we publish

Long-form guides built around a single decision: how to choose a sofa, how to size a rug, how to light a room. Each guide explains the reasoning and the measurements, so you can apply it to your own space rather than copying a list.

How we research

We work from manufacturer specifications, published care guidance, and standard interior practice. Where we quote a measurement, it is a range that holds for most homes, and we say when it does not. We do not claim to have tested products we have not handled.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through one we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. Commissions do not decide what we publish, and we do not accept payment for a positive recommendation. See our affiliate disclosure.
